How it works

Save your card once. Pay with just a code and a PIN.

The vendor's phone does the talking to our servers. You just need your code and your PIN, that's it.

01

Save your card once

Add your card while you have wifi. You get a permanent four-letter MoneyPadi code to share with vendors, and there's nothing to top up.

02

Vendor requests payment

The driver or vendor types in what you owe on their own connected device, e.g ₦200 for shuttle, or ₦1,000 for a haircut, whatever the price is.

03

You confirm with your PIN

Type your PIN on their screen to authorize it. Nobody can charge your card through MoneyPadi without it, and every charge is capped.
